# Emergency Trauma & First Aid Triage

During medical emergency response when professional help is unavailable, act according to MARCH protocol:

## MARCH Protocol
1. **M - Massive Bleeding**:
   - Apply direct firm pressure with clean cloth or sterile gauze.
   - Use tourniquet 2-3 inches above wound on extremities if severe arterial bleeding occurs. Note application time.
2. **A - Airway**:
   - Check if casualty is conscious and breathing. Ensure head-tilt, chin-lift unless spinal trauma is suspected.
3. **R - Respiration**:
   - Inspect chest for open wounds or unequal rise. Seal chest wounds with airtight material taped on 3 sides.
4. **C - Circulation**:
   - Check pulse, skin color, and temp. Keep victim warm with emergency blankets to prevent hypothermia and shock.
5. **H - Hypothermia & Head Injuries**:
   - Immobilize neck/head. Wrap in insulated blankets.

## Essential First Aid Supplies
- Sterile gauze, elastic bandages, adhesive tape, trauma shears.
- Antiseptic wipes (povidone-iodine / isopropyl alcohol).
- Oral Rehydration Salts (ORS) & electrolyte powders.
- Pain relievers (Acetaminophen/Paracetamol, Ibuprofen), antihistamines, broad-spectrum antibiotics (if prescribed).
- Burn gel / petroleum jelly.
