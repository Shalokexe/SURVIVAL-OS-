# Power & Off-Grid Lighting Solutions

When grid power fails, maintaining lighting and communications battery reserves is paramount.

## 1. Battery & Power Station Management
- Reserve power bank capacity primarily for low-power communications (phones, radio) rather than high-drain lighting.
- Keep Li-ion batteries between 20% and 80% charge level for long-term health when stored.
- Use 12V Deep Cycle AGM or LiFePO4 batteries with solar charge controllers for sustainable long-term off-grid setups.

## 2. Low-Energy Lighting Strategy
- Standard LED headlamps on lowest setting consume under 0.5 Watts (lasting 50+ hours on 3xAAA).
- Diffuse flashlight beams using water jugs/bottles to illuminate whole rooms efficiently.
- Candles & Oil Lamps: Place on non-flammable surfaces away from drafts. *Keep fire extinguishers or sand buckets nearby.*

## 3. Solar Panel Orientation & Care
- Angles: Tilt solar panels toward South in northern hemisphere (North in southern hemisphere) at angle = latitude + 15 degrees in winter.
- Clean dust, ash, or dew regularly off panel glass surfaces for maximum wattage efficiency.
