# Disaster Evacuation & Bug-Out Route Planning

Evacuation decisions must be made early before main transit corridors lock up or become hazards.

## 1. Decision to Evacuate vs. Shelter-in-Place
- **Shelter in Place**: Recommended during chemical spills, radiation plumes, extreme weather (cyclones/blizzards), or civil unrest where home structure remains sound.
- **Evacuate**: Necessary during rising flood waters, wildfire progression, structural instability, or mandatory authority orders.

## 2. Bug-Out Bag (72-Hour Survival Kit)
- 3 Liters water per person + purification tablets.
- Compact high-calorie rations (energy bars, freeze-dried food).
- First aid kit, knife/multi-tool, duct tape, 550 paracord.
- Waterproofed physical copies of IDs, emergency contacts, local offline paper map.
- Power bank, flashlight, emergency foil blanket, whistle.

## 3. Primary & Secondary Route Planning
- Identify 3 distinct routes out of your district (North, South, East/West).
- Avoid low-lying bridge choke points during flood hazards.
- Mark verified fuel stops and water points on your local survival map.
