# Emergency Water Purification & Filtration

Water is the single most critical resource in any emergency. An adult requires a minimum of 3 to 4 Liters of water daily for drinking, hygiene, and basic food preparation.

## 1. Boiling (Most Reliable Method)
- **Process**: Bring water to a rolling boil for at least 1 full minute (3 minutes at altitudes above 2,000 meters / 6,500 feet).
- **Effectiveness**: Kills all bacteria, viruses, protozoa, and parasites.
- **Limitation**: Does NOT remove chemical toxins, heavy metals, or particulate radiation.

## 2. Chemical Disinfection (Bleach / Iodine)
- **Unscented Household Bleach (5-9% Sodium Hypochlorite)**:
  - Add 2 drops of bleach per 1 Liter of clear water (4 drops if water is cloudy).
  - Stir thoroughly and let stand covered for at least 30 minutes.
  - Water should have a slight chlorine scent. If not, repeat dose once and wait 15 minutes.
- **Iodine Tablets / Tincture (2%)**:
  - Add 5 drops per Liter of clear water (10 drops if cloudy). Wait 30 minutes.
  - *Caution*: Do not use continuously for more than 3 weeks; avoid during pregnancy.

## 3. Solar Water Disinfection (SODIS)
- Fill transparent PET plastic bottles (up to 2L) with clear water.
- Place horizontally on a reflective surface (e.g. tin roof) in direct sunlight for 6 hours (2 days if cloudy).
- UV rays and heat destroy pathogens.

## 4. DIY Charcoal & Sand Layer Filter
For turbid/muddy water, filter before boiling:
1. Top layer: Fine gravel/pebbles (filters large debris).
2. Middle layer: Fine sand (filters micro-particles).
3. Bottom layer: Crushed activated charcoal (adsorbs organic compounds and improves odor).
4. Strain through cloth into clean container. *Always boil or chemically treat afterwards.*

## 5. Rainwater Harvesting Caution
- Collect from clean roofs (metal or tile preferred; avoid asphalt/lead flashing).
- Discard first 10 minutes of rainfall (roof flush).
- Treat all collected rainwater prior to consumption.
